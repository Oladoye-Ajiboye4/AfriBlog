Amara
Okafor

Amara Okafor is an essayist and cultural critic exploring the intersections of contemporary African literature, digital archiving, and post-colonial identity. Her work seeks to dismantle traditional narratives by highlighting the vibrant, evolving voices of the diaspora.

With a background in Comparative Literature from the University of Ibadan, she brings a meticulous, analytical lens to modern storytelling, treating everyday cultural artifacts with the reverence of historical texts.
